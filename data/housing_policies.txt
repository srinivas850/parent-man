HOUSING POLICIES FOR PARENTS

Housing Options:
- Traditional Residence Halls: Double and single rooms
- Suite-Style Housing: Shared bathroom and common area
- Apartment-Style Housing: Kitchen facilities included
- Off-Campus Housing: University-approved apartments
- Theme Housing: Special interest communities

Housing Application Process:
- Application Deadline: May 1 for fall semester
- Priority given to incoming freshmen and transfers
- Lottery system for returning students
- Deposit required to secure housing ($200  )
- Refundable within specified timeframe

Room Assignment Policies:
- Gender-inclusive housing available
- Quiet hours: 10 PM - 10 AM weekdays, midnight weekends
- Guest policies: 48-hour limit, must be registered
- Room changes: Allowed after first semester with approval
- Maintenance requests: Submit through online portal

Housing Costs:
- Traditional Hall: $6,500 per semester
- Suite Housing: $7,200 per semester
- Apartment Housing: $8,000 per semester
- Meal Plans: Required for residence hall students
- Optional for apartment students

Meal Plan Options:
- Unlimited Plan: $4,500 per semester
- 14-Meal Plan: $3,200 per semester
- 10-Meal Plan: $2,800 per semester
- Declining Balance: $500 initial deposit

Housing Rules and Regulations:
- No smoking in any university housing
- Alcohol policies: 21+ only, no kegs or common area drinking
- Pet policy: Fish only, no other pets allowed
- Decoration policies: No permanent changes to walls
- Noise ordinances must be followed

Safety and Security:
- 24/7 security presence in residence halls
- Key card access to buildings
- Emergency call boxes throughout campus
- Resident assistants on each floor
- Fire safety inspections monthly

Special Housing Needs:
- Accessibility accommodations available
- Medical housing for health-related needs
- Quiet housing for study needs
- Substance-free housing option
- Gender-neutral housing available

Housing Appeals and Grievances:
- Submit appeal within 10 days of assignment
- Include supporting documentation
- Reviewed by housing appeals committee
- Final decision within 14 days

Off-Campus Housing Resources:
- University-approved landlord list
- Housing search assistance
- Lease review service
- Off-campus student handbook
- Transportation services to off-campus housing
