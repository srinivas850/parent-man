FINANCIAL AID POLICIES FOR PARENTS

Types of Financial Aid:
- Grants: Free money that doesn't need to be repaid
- Scholarships: Merit-based or need-based awards
- Work-Study: Part-time jobs on campus
- Student Loans: Money borrowed that must be repaid with interest
- Parent PLUS Loans: Loans for parents to borrow for their child's education

FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid):
- Must be filed annually
- Deadline: March 1 for priority consideration
- Based on previous year's tax information
- Determines eligibility for federal aid programs

CSS Profile:
- Required by some private universities
- Additional financial information beyond FAFSA
- Used for institutional aid decisions
- Separate application and fee required

Cost of Attendance Breakdown:
- Tuition: $45,000 per year
- Room and Board: $12,000 per year
- Books and Supplies: $1,200 per year
- Transportation: $2,000 per year
- Personal Expenses: $3,000 per year
- Total: Approximately $63,200 per year

Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP):
- Must maintain minimum GPA (usually 2.0)
- Must complete 67% of attempted credits each semester
- Cannot exceed 150% of normal program length
- Failure to meet SAP results in loss of financial aid

Appeals for Financial Aid:
- Submit appeal form with supporting documentation
- Include explanation of circumstances (illness, family issues, etc.)
- May be granted probationary period to regain eligibility

Scholarship Opportunities:
- Academic Excellence Scholarships
- Athletic Scholarships
- Diversity Scholarships
- Community Service Scholarships
- Departmental Scholarships

Payment Plans:
- Monthly payment options available
- No interest charged for university payment plans
- Due dates align with semester billing cycles
