ACADEMIC POLICIES FOR PARENTS

Grade Point Average (GPA) Requirements:
- Minimum GPA of 2.0 required to remain in good standing
- Dean's List: 3.5 GPA or higher
- President's List: 4.0 GPA
- Academic Probation: Below 2.0 GPA
- Academic Suspension: Below 2.0 GPA for two consecutive semesters

Course Load Requirements:
- Full-time: 12-18 credits per semester
- Part-time: 6-11 credits per semester
- Maximum: 21 credits per semester (with advisor approval)
- Minimum: 12 credits for financial aid eligibility

Withdrawal Policies:
- Course Withdrawal: "W" grade, no impact on GPA
- Complete Withdrawal: "WD" grade for all courses
- Medical Withdrawal: "WM" grade for documented medical reasons
- Refund deadlines vary by date of withdrawal

Academic Integrity:
- Plagiarism: Using others' work without citation
- Cheating: Unauthorized assistance on assignments/exams
- Consequences: Warning, failing grade, suspension, expulsion
- Appeal process available for disputed cases

Attendance Policies:
- Professors set individual attendance requirements
- Excessive absences may result in course failure
- Make-up work at professor's discretion
- Documented absences (illness, family emergency) may be excused

Tutoring and Academic Support:
- Free tutoring center for all subjects
- Writing Center for essay assistance
- Math Lab for quantitative courses
- Supplemental Instruction for challenging courses

Academic Advising:
- Assigned advisor for each student
- Mandatory advising each semester
- Course selection and degree planning
- Academic progress monitoring

Grade Appeals:
- Submit appeal within 30 days of grade posting
- Include supporting documentation
- Reviewed by department chair and dean
- Final decision binding

Transfer Credit Policies:
- Maximum 90 credits transferable
- Minimum grade of C for transfer eligibility
- Course equivalency determined by registrar
- May require departmental approval for major courses
