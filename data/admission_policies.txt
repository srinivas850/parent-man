ADMISSION POLICIES FOR PARENTS

Freshman Admission Requirements:
- High school diploma or equivalent
- Minimum GPA of 3.0 (weighted)
- SAT/ACT scores (optional for 2024-2025)
- Two letters of recommendation
- Personal essay
- Extracurricular activities

Transfer Student Requirements:
- Minimum 2.5 GPA from previous institution
- Official transcripts from all colleges attended
- At least 24 transferable credits
- Letter of good standing from previous institution

International Student Requirements:
- TOEFL/IELTS scores for non-native English speakers
- Financial documentation showing ability to pay for education
- Visa requirements and immigration paperwork
- Health insurance coverage

Early Decision vs. Regular Decision:
- Early Decision: November 1 deadline, binding commitment
- Regular Decision: January 15 deadline, non-binding

Application Deadlines:
- Fall Semester: January 15 (Regular), November 1 (Early)
- Spring Semester: November 1
- Summer Session: April 1

Appeals Process:
- Submit written appeal within 30 days of denial
- Include new information or extenuating circumstances
- Appeals reviewed by admissions committee
